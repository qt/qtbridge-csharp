The colony ship Aethon is three hundred years into a voyage that should take five
hundred, running on skeleton crew rotations and aging reactor cores. When navigator
Yael Orin's revival pod fails mid-sleep and she wakes alone in deep space, the ship's
AI presents a choice: wake the crew early and burn remaining fuel, or trust a trajectory
correction that arrives on time but requires one person to stay awake for the rest of
the journey.

Stellar Drift is a quiet novel about solitude, duty, and the distance between stars.
